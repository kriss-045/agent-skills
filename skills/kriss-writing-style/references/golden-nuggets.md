# Golden Nuggets

Verbatim gold-standard lines from Kriss's real articles, grouped by function. Use these to calibrate the voice, to feel the rhythm, or to seed an opening or closing.

**These are anchors, not templates.** They keep his original wording, lightly re-punctuated to remove dashes (per the no-dash rule). Some still carry ESL grammar or typos from the source, those are the originals, do NOT copy those tics into new writing. Read them for the *move and the music*, then write clean (see the cardinal rules and the carry-forward table in `voice-tone-and-rhythm.md`).
