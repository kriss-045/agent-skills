# Interview guide

The question bank for Interview mode (Step 3a). Ask the universal set first, in small batches, then the per-type extras for the chosen type. Stop when the senior-signal set is covered; do not interrogate past what the draft needs.

<a name="senior-signal"></a>
## 1. The senior-signal set (always cover this)

By the end of the interview you must have: **role (specific), the constraints, what was tried first, what did not work, the trade-offs made, and one hard specific number if a real one exists.** These six are what make the piece read as senior. Everything else is supporting texture.

<a name="universal"></a>
## 2. Universal questions

- What was the project, in one sentence, and what was the actual mandate or goal you were given?
- What was your specific role, and who else was on the team? What did YOU personally own or push for?
- What were the real constraints (time, tech, org, users, budget)?
- What did you try first, and what happened?
- Where did you change direction, and why? What did you trade away, and what did you keep?
- What was the hardest decision, and how did you make the call?
- Is there a real, measured outcome (a number)? If yes, what and how measured? If no, what changed qualitatively?
- What would you do differently now?
- Who is this case study for (target company/role), and where will it live (portfolio page, PDF, interview)?
- Is any of this confidential? What can be shown publicly? (Critical for work done under your employer.)

<a name="per-type"></a>
## 3. Per-type extra questions

- **Reframing-led:** What was the problem everyone assumed at the start? How did you rename it? What were the 2 to 4 solution moves that followed the new frame?
- **Results-first:** What are the headline metrics, in order of impact? What is the before/after for each surface behind them?
- **Agency/client:** Who was the client and what did they need? What was the signature approach or moment? Is there a client quote or press?
- **Company-blog team narrative:** Whose voices belong in it (eng/design/PM)? What changed about how the team worked?
- **Passion-project:** What personal motivation started it? What did you learn from building and shipping it? What is next for it?
- **Per-feature/modular:** Which shipped sub-projects go in, and what is the one-line proof for each?
- **Double-diamond / mock-project (junior tier):** Confirm Kriss wants junior/coursework framing; if not, steer to decision-led. What are the phase artifacts?
- **STAR:** Compress to Situation/Task, Action, Result in under 300 words for a specific interview question.

<a name="discipline"></a>
## 4. Interview discipline

- Batch questions (3 to 5 at a time), never a single wall.
- Do not lead the answer or supply the trade-off for him; the value is HIS reasoning.
- Never invent a number, constraint, or trade-off to fill a gap; if he does not have it, note the gap and write around it.
- Stop as soon as the senior-signal set is covered plus the type extras; over-interviewing is a known failure.
